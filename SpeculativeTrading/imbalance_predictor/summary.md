# 2-Hour-Ahead Imbalance Predictor V2

## Overview

LightGBM quantile regression model predicting Slovak system imbalance (MWh) for each 15-minute settlement period, **2 hours before delivery**. Outputs point prediction (median) plus P10/P25/P75/P90 confidence intervals.

- **102 features** from 10 data sources (up from 55 in V1)
- **Train**: Jan 2024 -- Sep 2025 (59,598 samples)
- **Test**: Oct 2025 -- Jan 2026 (10,188 samples)
- **Target**: System Imbalance (MWh) per 15-min settlement period

## Data Leakage Audit

A comprehensive audit was performed. Two issues were found and fixed:

1. **Solar features (FIXED)**: Shift increased from 8 (2h) to 12 (3h) periods. Hourly solar actuals are only known after the hour ends; the original 2h shift could use data not yet published at prediction time.

2. **Load nowcast predictions (FIXED)**: The original `h2_predictions.csv` contained in-sample predictions from a model trained on ALL data (2024--2026). Replaced with walk-forward out-of-sample predictions generated by `generate_oos_predictions.py`, where each prediction comes from a model trained only on strictly prior data.

   Walk-forward folds:
   - Fold 1: Train [2024-01, 2025-01), Predict [2025-01, 2025-07)
   - Fold 2: Train [2024-01, 2025-07), Predict [2025-07, 2025-10)
   - Fold 3: Train [2024-01, 2025-10), Predict [2025-10, 2026-01)
   - Fold 4: Train [2024-01, 2026-01), Predict [2026-01, 2026-02)

   OOS nowcast MAE: 44.5 MW, correlation: 0.748 (vs in-sample which was unrealistically better).

3. **`nowcast_recent_bias` shift (FIXED)**: Changed from shift(8) to shift(12) to match DAMAS forecast error treatment, since the actual error component depends on realized load with publication delay.

All other feature groups confirmed safe by audit.

## Trading Strategy Backtest

### Setup

- **Bidirectional**: Sell IDM on surplus prediction, buy IDM on deficit prediction
- **Confidence-weighted sizing**: Position = min(|prediction|, 5) MWh
- **Settle at imbalance price**: P&L = (IDM_price - Imb_price) * size for sells, reversed for buys
- **Jan 19, 2026 price spike excluded** (imb_settlement_price > 5000 EUR/MWh)

### Results: |pred| > 2 MWh threshold, confidence-weighted 1--5 MWh

| Month | Trades | Win Rate | P&L (EUR) | EUR/day |
|-------|--------|----------|-----------|---------|
| Oct 2025 | 1,391 | 64% | +23,634 | +762 |
| Nov 2025 | 1,240 | 65% | +10,894 | +419 |
| Dec 2025 | 1,497 | 65% | +15,443 | +498 |
| Jan 2026 | 1,293 | 72% | +27,482 | +1,145 |
| **Total** | **5,421** | **66%** | **+77,454** | **+692** |

- **82 of 112 trading days profitable (73%)**
- Worst day: -1,050 EUR
- Annualized Sharpe: 8.6
- Confidence sizing 4.6x more profitable than flat 1 MWh sizing

### Strategy Variants

| Filter | Trades/day | Win Rate | P&L (EUR) | EUR/day | Sharpe |
|--------|-----------|----------|-----------|---------|--------|
| All predictions, weighted | 91 | 60% | +81,507 | +728 | 8.8 |
| \|pred\| > 2, weighted | 48 | 66% | +77,454 | +692 | 8.6 |
| \|pred\| > 5, weighted | 15 | 76% | +50,451 | +450 | 8.4 |

### Caveats

1. **Jan 2026 profit concentration**: Without the Jan 19 spike, Jan deficit trades earn ~4,000 EUR (vs +9,320 with spike). The spike contributed 57% of the original Jan deficit P&L. Base-rate edge is real but more modest.

2. **IDM-Imb structural spread**: In Oct--Dec 2025, a structural +7--9 EUR/MWh IDM>Imb spread existed (60% of periods). The model adds value on top of this, but part of the sell-side profit comes from the structural spread, not pure prediction. In Jan 2026 the spread flipped negative, and the model correctly adapted to trade the deficit side.

3. **Transaction costs not modeled**: IDM bid-ask spread, market impact, and settlement fees would reduce realized P&L.

4. **Production/Export features inactive**: These SCADA signals (Oct 2025+ only) have 0% importance because there's almost no training data. As more history accumulates, they should contribute since production directly enters the balance equation.

## Key Findings

1. **DA market data is the biggest unlock**: Cross-border flows (CZ, HU) and price changes added 17.4% feature importance -- all available D-1 with zero leakage risk.

2. **DAMAS forecast error autocorrelation is real signal**: The 24h rolling mean of forecast error is the single most important feature (3.5%), capturing systematic forecast bias that persists and drives imbalance.

3. **Market spreads encode trader positioning**: Lagged IDM VWAP and DA-IDM spread (8.8% combined) indicate how intraday traders are pricing the system, which correlates with subsequent imbalance.

4. **Walk-forward OOS nowcast predictions add clean value**: 4.8% importance from genuine out-of-sample load forecast error predictions. The model improved when switching from leaky in-sample to clean OOS predictions (64.7% vs 63.9% direction accuracy), suggesting the in-sample predictions were adding overfitted noise.

5. **Confidence-weighted sizing is highly effective**: Scaling position size with prediction magnitude (capped at 5 MWh) multiplied P&L 4.6x vs flat sizing while maintaining Sharpe ratio. The model's calibration -- bigger predictions are more likely correct -- is the foundation of this gain.
